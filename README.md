# Check the agent's call

An unofficial design concept by Gaurav Basantani for Overwatch Data: a fraud agent says block at 91%, but one of its three reasons is out of date; the analyst sees the evidence right next to the call and confirms, sets it aside or overrides. It is not affiliated with Overwatch Data, and the bank, people, case and numbers are made up and simplified. Live page: https://gauravbasantani.github.io/overwatch-concept/
