Design concept · Fraud investigation

Check the agent's call

A case view for bank fraud analysts, built on Overwatch Data's idea: agents take an investigation to a suggested decision, with the evidence an analyst needs to check it.

Gaurav Basantani
gauravbasantani.com
1The problem

Agents can work a fraud case in seconds, but a bank analyst still signs off and needs to see why.

2The idea

Show the case as a timeline with the evidence the agent used, right next to its suggested decision.

3What changes

The analyst confirms or overrides in under a minute, and every override teaches the agent.
